Date analyzed: 2026-10-14   Analyst: Claude Code (for Jorge Davalos)

and `docs/lab-pdfs/004 REGISTRO DE ENSAYOS A 7 DÍAS.pdf` (test date 09/09/2026). Same
form, both scanned and filled by hand.

## Purpose of the document

Lab worksheet filled in at the press **per obra and per test date**. It lists every
specimen of that obra broken that day, grouped by cast element. Signed by the Jefe de
laboratorio and the Coordinador del SGC. Its results complete the Bitácora
(general_flow.png: "genera información relevante para completar bitácora").

The 05/09 sheet mixes two elements: "Zapata de cimentación… apoyo no. 1 — (08:30 /
10:20)" (specimens 389, 390, 393, 394 at **7** days, so a pour on 29/08) and "Muro
adicional… — 10:30" (405, 406 at **3** days). So a sheet is not one record or one age.

## Relationships & cardinalities

- One sheet = one obra + one test date. N specimens from different records/samples.
- Every specimen appears on exactly one sheet: one test per specimen (Q-16).
- Tiempo / velocidad / tipo de falla are filled only for the first specimen of some
  groups (Q-63, Q-60).

## Calculated values

Nothing is printed as a formula. The printed numbers are consistent with these.
They are **observations, not formulas** (TODO: VALIDATE WITH LAB DOCUMENTATION):

| Value | Consistent with | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Prom. (height, diameter) | mean of the two readings, rounded half-up to 0.1 cm | 29.7/29.8 → 29.8; 15.1/15.0 → 15.1 |
| Área | π/4 × (rounded mean diameter)², 1 decimal | 14.9 → 174.4; 15.1 → 179.1 (not 15.05 → 177.9) |
| Newton | kgf × 9.81, rounded to an integer | 36 470 × 9.81 = 357 770.7 → 357 771 |
| Resistencia | kgf ÷ área, rounded to an integer | 36 470 ÷ 174.4 = 209.1 → 209 |
| % de resistencia | rounded resistencia ÷ resistencia de proyecto × 100 | 212 ÷ 250 = 84.8 |
| Velocidad | resistencia ÷ tiempo | 301 ÷ 114 = 2.64 |

Standard cited on FPC06-04 for this test: NMX-C-083-ONNCCE-2014 (cabeceo NMX-C-109-ONNCCE-2013).

## Workflow states / signatures / approvals observed

Two signatures per sheet. No per-test state.

## Business rules

- Test date = pour date + age, checked on all six specimens of the muro and the four of
  the zapata. 05/09/2026 is a Saturday, so tests run on weekends (A-13 partial).
- "Ecuación de corrección de carga" depends on the press calibration (Q-75).

## Validation rules

"Tolerancia edad" implies an allowed window around the planned age (Q-62).

## Numbering / identifiers

Specimen keys 389, 390, 393, 394, 405–408. The 391/392 gap suggests they are tested at
another age (consistent with 2+2 per sample, Q-14).

## Report requirements

If the lab needs to keep printing this sheet, it's a derived printable view over
SpecimenTests (project + test date), like the Bitácora (Q-69).

## Conflicts with current model

1. `SpecimenTest.performedAt` (timestamp) is replaced by `testDate` (date). The sheet
   records only the date.
2. Specimen dimensions are measured **at test time**, so they live on SpecimenTest, not
   on Specimen.
3. Tipo de falla for 407 is "3" here but "-" on FPC06-04 (Q-60).
